Leafy Living

Website Planning Document

1. Site Name

Leafy Living

Reason: This name represents a focus on vibrant, indoor plant life and appeals to beginners looking to bring nature into their living spaces. It is friendly, memorable, and clearly communicates the site's niche to college students and young professionals. The domain could hypothetically be leafylivingguide.com.

2. Site Purpose

An informational website created to help people who are new to having indoor plants take care of easy-to-maintain plants. The website will serve as a comprehensive guide for plant owners, giving them clear and useful advice on choosing the right plants, taking care of them, and fixing common problems that might come up. It focuses on three specific beginner-friendly plants to prevent users from feeling overwhelmed.

3. Scenarios

These are the specific questions our target audience will ask when visiting the site:

  1. Scenario 1 (The Dorm Student): "I live in a college dorm with very little natural sunlight; which of your recommended plants will survive in my room, and how often do I need to water it?"
  2. Scenario 2 (The Troubleshooter): "The leaves on my new houseplant are turning yellow with brown tips—what is causing this, and what steps can I take to save my plant?"
