Website Planning Document

Site Name

Mountain Trail Hikers

This name represents a community and resource hub for hiking enthusiasts focused specifically on exploring mountain trails. It is descriptive, easy to remember, and clearly communicates the site's niche to the target audience. The domain could hypothetically be mountaintrailhikers.com.

Site Purpose

The purpose of this website is to provide a comprehensive resource for hikers of all skill levels. It will offer detailed trail maps, safety tips for high-altitude environments, gear recommendations, and a community forum for members to share their hiking experiences, photos, and trail condition reports.

Scenarios

  • Scenario 1: A beginner hiker wants to find a mountain trail that is not too strenuous and wants to know what essential gear to pack for a day trip.
  • Scenario 2: An experienced hiker is looking for advanced, high-altitude trails in the region and wants to check recent trail condition reports submitted by other members before planning their weekend trip.

Color Scheme

The selected color palette reflects nature and the outdoors, fitting for a hiking website.

  • Dark Green (#2E5A36): Used for all headings (h1, h2, h3) and the main header background to represent forest foliage.
  • Earthy Brown (#8B5A2B): Used for the footer background and secondary accents to represent dirt and trails.
  • Off-White (#F8F9FA): Used for the main document background to ensure readability.
  • Charcoal (#333333): Used for paragraph text to provide strong contrast against the light background.

Typography

Two web-safe Google Fonts have been selected for readability and aesthetic appeal.

  • Merriweather (Serif): Applied to all headings (h1, h2, h3). The serif style gives a classic, outdoorsy, and established feel to the titles.
  • Open Sans (Sans-serif): Applied to all body text, paragraphs, and lists. It is highly legible on screens and provides a clean, modern contrast to the headings.
