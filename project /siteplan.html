1. Site Name

Leafy Living

This name represents a focus on vibrant, indoor plant life and appeals to beginners looking to bring nature into their living spaces. It is friendly, memorable, and clearly communicates the site's niche to college students and young professionals. The domain could hypothetically be leafylivingguide.com.

2. Site Purpose

An informational website created to help people who are new to having indoor plants take care of easy-to-maintain plants. The website will serve as a comprehensive guide for plant owners, giving them clear and useful advice on choosing the right plants, taking care of them, and fixing common problems that might come up. It focuses on three specific beginner-friendly plants to prevent users from feeling overwhelmed.

3. Scenarios

  • Scenario 1 (The Dorm Student): A college student living in a dorm room with low natural light wants to know which of the featured plants will survive in their space and how often they need to water it so it doesn't die.
  • Scenario 2 (The Troubleshooter): A young professional notices the leaves on their new houseplant are turning yellow with brown tips. They visit the site to use the troubleshooting section to find out what is causing the issue and how to fix it.

5. Typography

Two web-safe Google Fonts have been selected for readability and aesthetic appeal.

Merriweather (Serif)

Applied to all headings (h1, h2, h3). The serif style gives a classic, established, and organic feel to the titles, fitting for a nature-focused site.

The Quick Brown Fox Jumps Over the Lazy Dog

Open Sans (Sans-serif)

Applied to all body text, paragraphs, and lists. It is highly legible on screens and provides a clean, modern, and friendly contrast to the headings.
